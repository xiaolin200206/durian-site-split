# Highlights

- Random splits gave 1.6–2.3× the unseen-farm mAP50 of durian detectors
- The held-out farm dominated; mean detector scores differed by at most 0.022 mAP50
- Psyllid classes kept 25–30% of their AP on a new farm; other classes kept 48–59%
- At fixed training length, more farms and more images gave indistinguishable gains
- Fine-tuning on a few new-farm images alone cut accuracy; retraining with ~50 helped
