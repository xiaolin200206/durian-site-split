# Highlights

- Random splits gave 1.6–2.3× the unseen-farm mAP50 of six durian detectors
- The held-out farm set the score; detector means differed by at most 0.022 mAP50
- More farms and more images per farm both helped, by indistinguishable amounts
- Fine-tuning on a few new-farm images alone cut accuracy; retraining with ~50 helped
- Four predictions locked before scoring all held on a farm collected afterwards
