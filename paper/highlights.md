# Highlights

- Detectors scored 1.6–2.3 times lower mAP50 on durian farms never seen in training
- The held-out farm set the score: six detectors differed by ≤ 0.022 mAP50 on average
- Psyllid classes kept 25–30% of their AP on a new farm; other classes kept 48–59%
- With training length fixed, more farms and more images per farm helped about equally
- Fine-tuning on a new farm's images alone hurt accuracy; retraining with them helped
