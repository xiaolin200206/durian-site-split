Prospective test, site: Malacca (third of a planned multi-state series: Pahang, Penang, Malacca, Johor).
Written and locked BEFORE the farm was visited, together with the prelocks of the other remaining sites.

FARM
- One commercial durian farm in Malacca, arranged by the author; not previously visited. Visited by the author after this
  prelock was written; the actual date and the farm's district are recorded in the lock note.
- Novelty rule, fixed before the visit: the farm must not be any farm already known to have
  contributed images to the corpus (the nine peninsular sites of the main collection, in
  Selangor, Negeri Sembilan and the Muar district of Johor, including the eight farms of
  the analysis pool; the two Sabah orchards; Seremban; Pahang; and any site of this series
  visited earlier). If it turns out to be one of them, this site is EXCLUDED from
  the prospective test (its images may still be used for model training), and the exclusion
  and its reason are reported.

CAPTURE
- About 20 trees, numbered t1, t2, ... in walking order; number of photographs per tree
  not limited; hand-held, natural light, same phone as the main collection
  (iPhone 16 Pro Max), photographed through the SnapDurian PWA camera input.
- Tree assignment, fixed before the visit: odd-numbered trees (t1, t3, t5, ...) form
  THIS prospective test set; even-numbered trees (t2, t4, ...) belong to a separate
  sequential-model-update study and are never scored here. If fewer than 20 trees are
  photographed, the same rule applies to whatever was photographed.
- Healthy (no visible symptom) trees are photographed and kept under the same rule.
- Capture pipeline as for Pahang: the PWA resizes each photograph in the browser to a
  longest side of 1600 px and re-encodes it as JPEG (quality 0.85), which removes EXIF;
  the server stores that image after one further JPEG re-encode (quality 0.92). Resized
  to 640 x 640 before scoring.
- Location: the PWA's location setting is switched on; latitude/longitude are stored per
  photograph in the server database (not in the image file).
- Tree identity: recorded during the walk as time ranges per tree; after export, filenames
  are rewritten to encode orchard and tree (ml1t1_..., ml1t2_, ...). Each tree's
  first frame is a separator shot.
- Difficult views (small targets, leaf undersides, side leaves) are deliberately included
  in the same walk, as for Pahang; this can only lower the farm's score.
- All photographs of the assigned trees are annotated; none are set aside.

ANNOTATION
- Pre-labelled by the model active on the SnapDurian server on the visit day, planned to
  be v2 (v1's training set plus all Penang images, same recipe). That model does not exist when this prelock is written; its SHA-256 is
  recorded at the lock step, before scoring. The 200 LOFO models that are scored are NOT
  the pre-labelling model.
- The PWA detector uses an 11-class product label space. Every leaf_spot_blight /
  web_blight pre-label is reassigned by hand to Phomopsis or Leaf_rot (that class covers
  both paper classes); the preparation script refuses unresolved boxes.
- Pre-labels of 'scale_insect' on the upper leaf surface are relabelled Psyllid; on the
  underside they remain scale insect and are removed.
- Corrections are logged per image (boxes added / removed / relabelled) and reported as a
  correction rate in the lock note.
- A random subset of at least 40 images is additionally annotated from scratch with model
  output hidden; agreement with the corrected labels will be reported.
- Class rule for white waxy insects on the leaf upper surface: labelled Psyllid
  (indistinguishable from scale insects in a photograph; same control).
- Boxes of classes outside the six paper classes (scale insect, root disease, pink disease,
  stem borer, weevil, weevil damage) are removed; the images are kept.

PREDICTIONS (identical to Seremban and Pahang; scored with the same 200 frozen LOFO models)
- P1. YOLO11n (deployed architecture) scores below 0.3255 mAP50, the midpoint of its
      unseen-farm (0.223) and random-split (0.428) figures.
- P2. YOLO11n lies within the range of the eight held-out farms, 0.094-0.356.
- P3. The five detectors' means differ by at most 0.05 mAP50.
- P4. If both psyllid classes are present, at least one is among the two lowest-AP
      classes for YOLO11n.
- Aggregation: mAP50 over classes present on the farm, seeds averaged within each
  held-out-farm model set, then the eight sets averaged.
