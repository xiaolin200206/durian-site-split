Prospective test, site: Pahang (first of a planned multi-state series: Pahang, Penang, Malacca, Johor; Perak if time allows).
Written and locked BEFORE the farm was visited.

CAPTURE
- One commercial durian farm in Pahang, visited by the author after this prelock was
  written; the actual date is recorded in the lock note.
- About 20 trees, numbered t1, t2, ... in walking order; number of photographs per tree
  not limited; hand-held, natural light, same phone as the main collection
  (iPhone 16 Pro Max), photographed through the SnapDurian PWA camera input.
- Tree assignment, fixed before the visit: odd-numbered trees (t1, t3, t5, ...) form
  THIS prospective test set; even-numbered trees (t2, t4, ...) belong to a separate
  sequential-model-update study and are never scored here. Alternating in walking
  order spreads both sets evenly across the farm and the day. If fewer than 20 trees
  are photographed, the same rule applies to whatever was photographed.
- Healthy (no visible symptom) trees are photographed and kept under the same rule.
- Capture pipeline differs from Seremban and is recorded here: the PWA resizes each
  photograph in the browser to a longest side of 1600 px and re-encodes it as JPEG
  (quality 0.85), which removes EXIF; the server stores that image after one further
  JPEG re-encode (quality 0.92). Seremban images were camera originals exported by
  Roboflow. Both are resized to 640 x 640 before scoring.
- Location: the PWA's location setting is switched on; latitude/longitude from the
  browser are stored per photograph in the server database (not in the image file).
  Capture time is the time the PWA processed the photograph.
- Tree identity: recorded during the walk as time ranges per tree; after export,
  filenames are rewritten to encode orchard and tree (p1t1_..., p1t2_, ...), as for
  Sabah (o1t1_...) and Seremban. Each tree's first frame is a separator shot.
- Unlike Seremban, difficult views (small targets, leaf undersides, side leaves)
  are deliberately included in the same walk. This can only LOWER the farm's score,
  i.e. it works in favour of P1 and against the upper end of P2; it is the opposite
  direction to the Seremban set-aside, which could only raise the score.
- All photographs of the assigned trees are annotated; none are set aside before
  annotation (unlike Seremban, where 23 of 105 were set aside as too ambiguous).

ANNOTATION
- Pre-labelled by the deployed PWA detector (v0; SHA-256 recorded in this file under
  prelabel_model), then corrected box by box by the author under the Section 2.2
  standard. The 200 LOFO models that are scored are NOT the pre-labelling model.
- The PWA detector uses an 11-class product label space. Its 'leaf_spot_blight' class
  covers both paper classes Phomopsis and Leaf_rot (on the Seremban set it was the
  detector's label for 82 of the matched Phomopsis boxes and 39 of the matched
  Leaf_rot boxes), so every leaf_spot_blight / web_blight pre-label is reassigned by
  hand to Phomopsis or Leaf_rot; the preparation script refuses unresolved boxes.
- Pre-labels of 'scale_insect' on the upper leaf surface are relabelled Psyllid
  (rule below); on the underside they remain scale insect and are removed.
- Corrections are logged per image (boxes added / removed / relabelled) and will be
  reported as a correction rate in the lock note.
- A random subset of at least 40 images is additionally annotated from scratch with
  model output hidden; agreement with the corrected labels will be reported.
- Class rule for white waxy insects on the leaf upper surface: labelled Psyllid
  (indistinguishable from scale insects in a photograph; same control); scale insects
  are only labelled as such when identifiable on the leaf underside.
- Boxes of classes outside the six paper classes (scale insect, root disease, pink
  disease, stem borer, weevil, weevil damage) are removed; the images are kept.
- Images resized to 640x640 (stretch) before scoring, as in the submitted paper.

PREDICTIONS (identical to Seremban; scored with the same 200 frozen LOFO models)
- P1. YOLO11n (deployed architecture) scores below 0.3255 mAP50, the midpoint of its
      unseen-farm (0.223) and random-split (0.428) figures.
- P2. YOLO11n lies within the range of the eight held-out farms, 0.094-0.356.
- P3. The five detectors' means differ by at most 0.05 mAP50.
- P4. If both psyllid classes are present, at least one is among the two lowest-AP
      classes for YOLO11n.
- Aggregation: mAP50 over classes present on the farm, seeds averaged within each
  held-out-farm model set, then the eight sets averaged.
